Volumetric Setup

Step 1: System Prep

This wizard measures your pump's exact flow rate, allowing you to schedule watering by precise volumes (mL) instead of timers.

Precision Note:

Due to the mechanical pulsing of diaphragm pumps and shifting reservoir levels, expect a real-world accuracy of ±1 to 3 mL per run.

Crucial: Your tubes must be entirely full of water. If there is air in the lines, place the hose in a bucket and tap below.

Step 2: Dispense Test (Pass 1 of 2)

  1. Place an empty cup on a digital kitchen scale.
  2. Tare the scale to exactly 0 grams.
  3. Place the cup beneath the Zone A nozzle.
  4. Tap the button below and scan your controller.

Wait for the pump to completely stop before clicking Next.

Step 3: Measure Weight

Weigh the water currently in the cup. Because 1 gram of water equals exactly 1 milliliter, enter the scale's reading directly below.
