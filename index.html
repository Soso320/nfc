Volumetric Calibration

Step 1: System Prep

Welcome to the Volume Calibration Wizard. This process measures your pump's true flow rate, allowing you to schedule watering by precise volumes (mL) instead of timers.

Precision Expectations:

Due to the physical pulsing of diaphragm pumps and changing water levels, this system achieves a real-world accuracy of ±1 to 3 mL per run.

Crucial: Your tubes must be completely full of water. If there is air in the lines, place the hose in a bucket and tap below to purge the system.

Step 2: Dispense Test (Pass 1 of 2)

  1. Place an empty cup on a digital kitchen scale.
  2. Tare the scale to 0 grams.
  3. Place the cup beneath the Zone A nozzle.
  4. Tap the button below and scan your controller.

Wait for the pump to completely finish its soft-stop before tapping next.

Step 3: Measure Weight

Weigh the water currently in the cup. Because 1 gram of water equals exactly 1 milliliter, you can enter the scale's reading directly below.
